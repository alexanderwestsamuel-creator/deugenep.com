This collection comes from the confusion I carried as a boy, trying to understand why masculinity and femininity were treated as opposites when I had always seen them living together. I never saw softness as something that weakened strength, or fluidity as something separate from structure.

Designing across extremes became my way of making sense of that tension. This work questions the idea of a singular masculinity, something I could never fully fit inside of, and instead embraces the complexity of existing between energies, between expectations, between definitions.
