Daxton Eugene Price

Daxton is a Utah-based designer, on-set tailor, and stylist working across original garments and editorial production.

His design language is rooted in naturalism and patina: the beauty created when material is allowed to age, soften, and fray.

Available for commissions, editorial work, on-set tailoring, styling, and travel.
